# SAT Archived

The OLD pre 1995 SAT is an immaculate test to proxy general cognitive ability and FSIQ due to its immense high g loading of 0.93 and large sample size of over 1 million test takers. For reference, the Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale (WAIS) is considered the flagship modern IQ test, with a g loading of 0.95. Therefore, the digitalized old SAT is considered one of the best contenders to get an accurate depiction of FSIQ that can be taken online or without in-person testing.

A 0.93 g loading means that, where g is the general factor of intelligence, it is correlated with g to a relationship of 93%.

Squaring the g loading of 0.93 yields to 0.8649, or 86.49%. This is r^2 - the coefficient of determination. This means that 86.49% of the differences in scores is directly explained by general intelligence.

This library consists of 37 old SAT forms, containing 5,359 questions. Each form contains its scoring table, answer key, and is correctly mapped to its PDF counterpart and checked extensively with AI and human review.
